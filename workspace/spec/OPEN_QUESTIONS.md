# Open questions: meeting-room booking (example)

Not decided. The model draws the documented alternatives and marks them OPEN; it never picks one.

| Id | Question | Options | Touches |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q-01 | The person confirms and no answer comes back (the connection drops). Is the hold still theirs while the outcome is unknown? | (a) The hold is kept until its period ends, and the person can confirm again. (b) The hold is given up, and the person starts again from the list. | D-08, D-10, AC-J01-11 |
| Q-02 | May a booking that has already started be changed? | (a) No: a started booking can only end early. (b) Yes, it can be made longer if the room is free afterwards. | D-12, D-19 |
| Q-03 | How long is the grace period before a booking with no check-in is released? | A number of minutes for PN-03. | D-16, PN-03 |
